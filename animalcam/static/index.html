animalcam collects moments from live wildlife cameras. Luna looks for animals and birds; Kiwi adds sourced facts about the species we spot.

  • Checks run every 15 minutes. Check now requests a fresh frame and analysis.
  • The gallery keeps your captures. Filters help you find species, birds, and interesting moments.
  • Stats count frames with sightings, not unique animals. AI identifications and counts can be mistaken.
